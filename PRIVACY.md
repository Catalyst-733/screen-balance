# Screen Balance privacy policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Screen Balance is a break-reminder and screen-time app for Windows. It is built to be private by design.

## What it collects

To show your screen time, Screen Balance records, on your PC only:

- the **name of the app** in the foreground (for example `chrome` or `Code`) and how many minutes you used it,
- your total screen time per day and per hour,
- your breaks (how many and how long),
- your settings.

It does **not** record window titles, web addresses, what you type, screenshots, files, or anything else you see or do inside an app.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. Everything is stored in one folder on your PC, `%USERPROFILE%\.screenbalance\`, and history older than 60 days is deleted automatically.

Screen Balance makes **no network connections**. It has no account, no analytics or telemetry, no ads, and it shares nothing with the developer or anyone else.

## Your control

- You can see all your data in the app's dashboard, and remove time you added by hand.
- To delete everything, quit the app and delete the `.screenbalance` folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder automatically, so your history is still there if you reinstall it; delete the folder if you want it gone.

## Children

Screen Balance doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
