## Product name
Screen Balance

## Short description (up to 100 characters)
Gentle break reminders and honest screen time, right from your system tray. Private and offline.

## Description
It's easy to sit down "for a minute" and look up three hours later. Screen Balance runs quietly in the system tray and gives you a gentle nudge at the right moments. It doesn't lock you out or nag you: it makes your screen time visible and reminds you to step away.

BREAK REMINDERS
After 50 minutes of continuous use, a small popup slides in. Snooze it, or start a break: a calm full-screen timer covers every monitor and suggests a quick stretch, rest for your eyes or a glass of water.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
The dashboard shows today's screen time, your breaks, your top apps and the last 7 days. Analytics and Apps tabs show when you use your PC and where your hours go, app by app.

A TRAY ICON THAT TELLS YOU HOW YOU'RE DOING
The hourglass fills up as you approach your daily limit, so you know where you stand without opening anything.

ONLY REAL USE COUNTS
Time away from the keyboard doesn't count, but watching a video does. Screen Balance respects Windows' Do not disturb and Focus, and has its own Do not disturb for meetings and games.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
100% offline: no account, no telemetry, no ads. It records only app names and minutes, never window titles, websites or keystrokes, and everything stays on your PC.

Light and dark mode, follows your Windows theme. Optional: start with Windows.

## "runFullTrust" capability justification (asked during submission)
Screen Balance is a classic desktop app. It needs full trust to read how long the PC has been
idle, which app is in the foreground and whether audio is playing, so it can count real screen
time and remind the user to take breaks. All data stays on the device.
